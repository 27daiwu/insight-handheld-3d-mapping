**Column notes.** ATE *aligned* = rigid (Umeyama) alignment before RMSE; *anchored* = no alignment (the Session/VIO world frame is used as is). Peak RSS = max resident set over the SLAM process tree. Map accuracy/completeness are distances to the map reconstructed from ground-truth poses with the same frames and fusion code (est→ref / ref→est). `vio_raw` = Insight VIO as recorded; `*_odom_only` = the odometry a back-end started from, before RTAB-Map's optimisation.

**Caveats (read before drawing conclusions).**
* Data: synthetic room (VIO = ground truth + injected smooth drift) and TUM fr1/desk, fr1/xyz (VIO = ground truth + the same drift model). The "VIO" input is therefore *synthetic*; the comparison shows how well each back-end removes a known smooth drift, not how it behaves on real Insight VIO.
* **Own-odometry rows are noisy.** Repeating `rtabmap_odom` 4× gave ATE(aligned) 0.030–0.032 m on the synthetic room, but 0.027–0.051 m on TUM desk, and in 1 of 4 TUM runs tracking was lost after ~100 frames (stock `Vis/MinInliers` 20 lost it in most runs). Defaults were tuned on these same sequences → optimistic.
* `rtabmap_vio` wall time (60–140 s) is dominated by the lock-step replay (one frame at a time, waiting for RTAB-Map's output), not by the algorithm; the custom back-end's 2–4 s is not comparable (different algorithm, no real-time replay).
* The in-repo custom back-end and `rtabmap_vio` are both fed the same drifted VIO; both reach 0.02–0.04 m. On the synthetic room GTSAM-optimised RTAB-Map is slightly worse than custom (0.041 vs 0.036), on TUM they are equal within noise (0.020 vs 0.018). No claim of superiority either way.
* Maps from `rtabmap_odom` trajectories are better than from `rtabmap_vio` on the synthetic room (accuracy median 0.021 vs 0.051 m) although ATE is similar; on the real-image TUM sequences both are equal. *Hypothesis, not verified*: after optimisation the VIO-based path keeps larger local (relative) error (RPE rotation ≈1.0° vs 0.35° raw), which hurts TSDF fusion more than global ATE shows.
